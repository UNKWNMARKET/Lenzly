## Summary

Complete first-version build of LENZLY, a dark photography platform for discovering photo spots, connecting photographers, and getting hired by brands.

### What's included

**Core App (React + Vite + TypeScript)**
- Feed, Explore, Find a Photographer, and Profile tabs
- Instagram-style photo feed with likes, bookmarks, and double-tap
- AI-discovered photo spots with 5-day weather forecasts (Open-Meteo API) and golden hour tips
- Live Leaflet map of photographers with dark CartoDB tile layer
- 50-state architecture spots with GPS, shooting tips, and weather data
- Florida spotlight: 19 spots across 11 cities with 8 local photographer profiles
- Brand portal for companies to discover and hire freelance photographers

**Admin Console**
- Express.js backend (port 3001) with JWT auth and bcrypt password hashing
- Full-screen admin layout bypassing the mobile 430px constraint
- Dashboard, Photo Spots, Photographers, Posts, Brands, and Settings pages
- Brand application review (approve / reject / delete)
- Live-editable app settings (name, color, pricing, maintenance mode, emails)

**Legal & Compliance**
- Privacy Policy (GDPR / CCPA / COPPA compliant)
- Terms of Service (14 sections)
- Trademark-clean brand names throughout

**iOS / App Store**
- Capacitor iOS wrapper (bundle ID: com.lenzly.app)
- Codemagic CI/CD workflow for cloud App Store builds
- Live on Vercel: https://lenzly-tau.vercel.app
